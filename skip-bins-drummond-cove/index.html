Skip hire in Drummond Cove

Drummond Cove is a coastal community north of Geraldton, with many holiday homes and older beach houses. Clean-outs of furniture, bedding and old outdoor gear are common, along with renovation waste. A 4–6 m³ skip suits most house clean-outs.

Sand and salt air

Coastal sand can be soft, so ask about boards under the bin. If your street is narrow, mention it so the operator can plan the drop-off.

Nearest tip

The City of Greater Geraldton's Meru Waste Management Facility is on Landfill Lane, off Goulds Road in Narngulu, about 10 km from the city centre. The City lists it as open daily 7:30am to 4:30pm, closed Christmas Day, New Year's Day and Good Friday. It has the Bowerbird recycling and reuse centre and a household hazardous waste area for paint, chemicals and batteries. Small amounts of residential asbestos are accepted if double-wrapped and marked "asbestos". Check the City of Greater Geraldton website for fees and accepted items.

Council rules

A skip wholly on your own land generally doesn't need approval. On the road or verge, the City of Greater Geraldton requires a Licence to Obstruct a Thoroughfare or Verge. See the verge licence guide.
